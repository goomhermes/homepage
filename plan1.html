The Core Idea

Not a course. A world they return to.

English is not taught in isolation — it is discovered inside a story. Children aren't "doing lessons." They're rescuing the Enchanted Library.

📖 The Story

Five small creatures find a broken Library in a magical forest. Its books lost their words in a mysterious storm. The creatures must find the lost words, learn them, and restore the Library page by page.

🧠 Why Narrative Works

7-to-9-year-olds absorb through meaning, emotion, and story — not isolated skills. Narrative creates intrinsic motivation, natural repetition, and embedded social-emotional learning.

🌍 The World

Each Chapter is one room of the Library. Each Lesson is a quest inside that room. Progress is visible — the world literally restores as children learn.

Chapter Architecture

Four Rooms. One Journey.

The Spiral Ramp — each Chapter revisits and deepens prior language at a higher altitude.

Weeks 1–4

🚪 Chapter 1: The Entrance Gate

The creatures meet. They learn each other's names and open the gate together.

  • Wk 1 Hello & Goodbye — greetings, introductions
  • Wk 2 Who Am I? — "I am ___", identity
  • Wk 3 What Do You See? — "I see a ___", basic nouns
  • Wk 4 Open the Gate! — review + milestone
🔑 Golden Key Gate Opening Ceremony
Weeks 5–9

🎨 Chapter 2: The Rainbow Room

Faded paintings on the walls. The creatures find lost color-words to restore them.

  • Wk 5 Colors in the Dark — color vocabulary
  • Wk 6 Big & Small — size words
  • Wk 7 What Color Is It? — questions + answers
  • Wk 8 I Like… / I Don't Like… — preferences
  • Wk 9 The Color Feast — creative review
🖌️ Painter's Brush Color Playground
Weeks 10–14

🌳 Chapter 3: The Family Tree

A giant tree with empty frames. The creatures fill in the people they love.

  • Wk 10 This Is My Family — family vocabulary
  • Wk 11 He Is… / She Is… — pronouns + identity
  • Wk 12 How Many? — numbers 1–10
  • Wk 13 Where Is…? — prepositions
  • Wk 14 Our Big Family — collaborative story
🍃 Memory Leaf Family Story Maker
Weeks 15–19

⏰ Chapter 4: The Daily Clock

A giant broken clock. Time is frozen. The creatures restart it with their daily routines.

  • Wk 15 Morning, Afternoon, Night — time of day
  • Wk 16 I Wake Up… — daily routine verbs
  • Wk 17 I Can… / I Can't… — ability
  • Wk 18 What Time Is It? — telling time
  • Wk 19 A Day in the Forest — full narration
🕰️ Golden Hour Hand My Perfect Day
🏆 Week 20: The Grand Re-Opening Ceremony

Library Passport revisit → Multi-chapter challenge → 30-second Librarian's Speech → Master Librarian Badge

Course 1 Blueprint 4 / 14
Motivation System

Narrative Incentive Layering

We reject extrinsic-only motivation. No competitive leaderboards. No lives that run out. No penalty for mistakes.

🌍

World Restoration

Every skill learned restores part of the Library. Colors return to paintings. The clock ticks. Progress is visible.

🦊

Companion Bond

Each child chooses a creature. It learns alongside them — makes mistakes, asks questions, celebrates growth. Emotional connection.

🗝️

Collectible Artifacts

Earned at each Chapter's end. Narrative meaning — each unlocks a bonus creative activity. Not random. Earned.

📘

Library Passport

A digital stamp book. By Week 20, every room is marked "restored." A visual record of the entire journey.

🚫 Deliberately Excluded: Time pressure · Competitive leaderboards · "Lives" system · Penalty for mistakes · Streaks that punish absence

Design Language

The App as a Living World

Not a series of screens — a continuous, explorable environment. Studio Ghibli meets Apple.

🎨

Art Style

Soft hand-painted watercolor. Paper texture. Warm palette. No harsh outlines.

✨

Animations

Physics-based. Elements have weight. Elastic lag. Gentle momentum. Nothing is instant.

🔊

Sound Design

Ambient forest soundscape per room. Satisfying tap responses. Professional child voice-overs.

📄

Transitions

3D page-fold metaphor. Pages have depth. Reinforces the "living book" throughout.

🧭 Navigation — No Menus, Only Doors

The Foyer — Home. Tap a creature to enter their chapter.

Chapter Rooms — Explorable spaces. Current lesson glows. Completed work becomes background scenery.

The Passport — Tap your creature companion. See your journey.

The Clearing — End-of-session meadow. Calm reflection.

🔤 Typography

Custom rounded sans-serif for ESL legibility. Korean in complementary style. L1 support toggleable, never forced.

Course 1 Blueprint 7 / 14
Lesson Deep-Dive

Anatomy of One Session

Session 7 — "What Color Is It?"   Chapter 2 Week 2

Core target: "What color is this?" / "It's [color]." — reinforced color vocabulary with phonics connection.

1

The Threshold

7 min

"Spotlight Hunt" — The Rainbow Room is dark. The child drags a flashlight across the screen, revealing hidden color fragments in the paintings. Pure discovery, zero pressure. Review of prior color vocabulary.

2

The Discovery

12 min

"The Question Mark Painting" — A blank canvas at the back of the room. The owl introduces "What color is this?" with words appearing one-by-one in light. Child repeats (voice recorded, layered with owl). Owl demonstrates with 3 colors. Then 6 mini-canvases for guided practice — child speaks, app recognizes, canvas bursts with color.

⚡

Movement Minute — "Stand up! Touch something RED! Something BLUE! Something GREEN!" — Physical reset.

3

The Workshop

15 min

"The Color Mixing Studio" — Drag-and-drop painting (elastic paint physics). 3 rounds: fill a flower (red petals, green stem), a butterfly (orange + purple, must speak before placing), a sunset (multi-color, full Q&A). Then "Color Detective" — riddles that lead to hidden objects in the room. Self-paced, no penalty.

4

The Stage

12 min

"The Rainbow Reporter" — Child becomes a journalist. Asks the fox "What color is this?", then reports to the audience. Voice gets a "reporter" audio filter. Generates a 5-second animated news clip. Repeat for 3 objects. Dual-screen mode available for pairs.

5

The Clearing

7 min

"Sunset in the Rainbow Room" — The creatures sit in a circle. Owl asks what color you like most. Child records their answer. A color droplet floats down → transforms into a paint palette for the Passport. Fox whispers a teaser for tomorrow's lesson.

🤖 Companion AI

  • ✓ Responds using only taught vocabulary
  • ✓ Personality-consistent (fox = energetic, owl = wise)
  • ✓ Hedgehog asks "Can you teach me?" — role flip
