DEEP DIVESample Lesson: "Hello, Lingo!"

Chapter 1, Session 1 — The very first lesson a child experiences.

Segment Time Activity
🔥 WARM-UP 3 min Animated intro — Lingo the owl flies in, asks "What's your name?" Child taps avatar to respond. Avatar customization unlocks.
📚 CORE WAVE 1 10 min Phonics: Short "a" — Letter "a" is a living character that stretches/compresses to show the /a/ sound. Drag items (apple, ant, cat) to match.
🏃 BREAK 2 min "Stretch like an A!" — Arms up in "A" shape, crunch for /a/. Mirror animation on screen.
🛠️ CORE WAVE 2 10 min Vocabulary: "Hello," "Hi," "My name is..." — Interactive dialogue with 3 animated characters. Real-time pronunciation feedback.
🏃 BREAK 2 min "Name Game Dance" — Rhythmic repetition of names to a beat. Tap to create rhythm patterns.
🎤 CORE WAVE 3 8 min Production: Create a mini-introduction video. Record voice, select background, add stickers. AI guides the creation.
🎉 CELEBRATION 5 min Explorer Badge awarded. Quick recap game. Preview of tomorrow's adventure. Animated credits with child's avatar.

INTERACTIVEMicro-Challenge Stations

Tactile, hands-on digital experiences that bridge physical and virtual learning.

🪣

Sound Sorting

Drag floating objects into phoneme buckets. Haptic feedback on correct placement. Objects "snap" into place.

🧱

Word Builder

Arrange letter tiles to form words. Letters glow when adjacent to correct neighbors. Wrong combos wiggle away.

🎬

Story Sequencer

Put illustrated scenes in order. Correct placement triggers animation. Wrong order = funny "out of sequence" reaction.

🎨

Voice Painter

Speak words to "paint" colors. Pitch and volume affect color and intensity. Creates unique, shareable artwork.

🌸

Memory Garden

Match word-image pairs. Each match blooms a flower in your virtual garden. Complete the garden to unlock secrets.

🎭

Role Play

Interactive dialogues with branching paths. Choose your response. Each choice leads to different story outcomes.
